Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity minimalist desktop website concept, landscape 1536×1024
Primary request: Create composition option B for “Long Weekend SG”, a simple one-page Singapore leave planner. It must look like a quiet, premium utility, not a themed product.
Structure: a centred narrow page header with exact title “Long Weekend SG” and subtitle “Plan the breaks worth taking.” Beneath it, one slim horizontal toolbar contains a 2027/2028 segmented selector, school-break switch, Saturday-policy switch, and tiny legend. The twelve white month cards form a clean four-column by three-row grid below. Each month card has a strong month name, tiny year label, Monday-first grid, and minimal status marks. One selected October card has a very subtle blue outline. A small anchored white popover overlaps its top edge and reads exactly “Take 1 day → rest 4 days”; two alternative combinations appear as compact text links.
Visual style: soft warm-white page background, pure-white cards, hairline neutral-grey borders, almost no shadows, black typography, small muted-grey labels, one restrained red for public holidays and powder blue for leave days. Generous spacing, crisp grid, calm and lightweight.
Constraints: no dark backgrounds, no airport styling, no travel imagery, no logo, no textures, no illustrations, no props, no gradients, no glass effects, no large hero, no heavy shadows, no decorative ticket motifs, no fake claims, no watermarks. Keep everything semantic-looking and directly buildable in HTML/CSS. The calendar interaction is the only expressive element.
