Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web app concept, landscape 1536×1024
Primary request: Create a clean, minimalist composition for “Long Weekend SG”, a one-page interactive Singapore public-holiday leave planner. This is a usable website screen, not a poster.
Structure: a small text masthead at the top left with exact title “Long Weekend SG” and one concise line: “Make your leave days go further.” At top right, a minimal segmented year control “2027 Official / 2028 Forecast” and two compact switches. Below, a four-column by three-row grid of twelve equal white month cards fills the page. One February card is selected. A simple white recommendation popover with a thin border floats just above that card and says exactly “Take 2 days → rest 5 days”, with two quiet alternative rows.
Content: Monday-first calendar grids for JAN through DEC, red circular public-holiday marks, pale blue suggested-leave highlights, very light school-break shading, a small legend and source footer. The purpose—scan the year and discover leave combinations—must be obvious.
Visual style: extremely clean modern utility. Soft off-white page background (#f6f7f8), pure white cards, 1px light-grey borders, subtle soft shadow only on hover/selected card, black and charcoal typography, one restrained Singapore red accent, one calm pale blue accent. Excellent spacing and typographic hierarchy, similar craft to a premium calendar or finance utility.
Constraints: no dark background, no airport theme, no travel imagery, no props, no textures, no decorative illustrations, no gradients, no glassmorphism, no heavy shadows, no oversized marketing hero, no generic bento dashboard, no fake claims, no logo mark, no watermark. Keep the page simple, flat, spacious, and fully buildable in semantic HTML/CSS.
