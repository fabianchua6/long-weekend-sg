Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop website composition concept, landscape 1536×1024
Primary request: Create composition option B for “Long Weekend SG”, a one-page interactive Singapore public-holiday leave planner. This is a designed web app screen, not a poster and not a photograph.
Structure: a left-side vertical operations rail occupying about 22% of the viewport carries the exact product name “LONG WEEKEND SG”, the exact headline “MAKE LEAVE WORK HARDER”, the year selector “2027 OFFICIAL / 2028 FORECAST”, two compact policy toggles, legend, and sources. The remaining right side is the planner: twelve individual month cards in a dense three-column by four-row field. A selected May card expands horizontally across two columns without becoming a generic modal. A perforated boarding-pass recommendation strip bridges from the operations rail into the selected month, with the exact message “TAKE 1 DAY → REST 4 DAYS” and two compact alternatives.
Content: real-looking Monday-first month grids, JAN through DEC, holiday markers, suggested leave dates, forecast/official status, optional school-holiday indicator. The purpose must read instantly as a leave planner.
Visual world: Changi-inspired planning desk without any Changi logo or trademark. Deep midnight airport navy ground, warm ivory paper cards, Singapore red accents, brushed aluminium rails, condensed departure-board numerals, operational micro-labels, tactile paper grain, perforations and clipped ticket corners. Premium, disciplined, legible.
Signature interaction shown as a still: one long illuminated continuous-break strip crosses the selected month and the ticket physically aligns with it.
Constraints: desktop web UI only; no people, planes, skyline, travel photography, fake testimonials, gradients, glassmorphism, bento-dashboard chrome, generic SaaS cards, logos, or watermarks. Keep UI buildable with semantic HTML/CSS and accessible controls. One dominant focal moment; quiet supporting months.
