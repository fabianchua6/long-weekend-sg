Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop website composition concept, landscape 1536×1024
Primary request: Create composition option C for “Long Weekend SG”, a one-page interactive Singapore public-holiday leave planner. This is a designed web app screen, not a poster and not a photograph.
Structure: a compact centred masthead at top with exact title “LONG WEEKEND SG” and subtitle “Stretch your leave. See the whole year.” Directly under it is a horizontal control strip styled like an airport gate panel: “2027 OFFICIAL”, “2028 FORECAST”, “SCHOOL BREAKS”, and “SATURDAY PH POLICY”. The twelve individual month cards appear below as four tall vertical departure slips in each of three horizontal seasonal bands, with JAN–APR, MAY–AUG, SEP–DEC. Month cards remain equally legible. A selected October-to-November break is the focal moment: highlighted dates continue between two adjacent month cards and a floating compact recommendation ticket hangs in the gap above them with exact text “TAKE 2 DAYS → REST 5 DAYS”, plus two alternative chips.
Content: Monday-first calendar grids, visible JAN through DEC, accurate-looking date density, public-holiday circles, bridge-day stamps, school-break shading, source and status footer. It must clearly function as a year-at-a-glance leave planner.
Visual world: Changi-inspired planning desk without any Changi logo or trademark. Deep midnight airport navy ground, warm ivory paper, Singapore red, brushed aluminium, condensed departure-board numerals, tiny operational labels, tactile paper grain, perforated edges. Premium and precise, not nostalgic kitsch.
Signature interaction shown as still: the continuous break behaves like one runway-guidance path linking adjacent cards; the popup ticket visually attaches to the path.
Constraints: desktop web UI only; no people, planes, skyline, travel photography, passports, pens, desk props, fake testimonials, gradients, glassmorphism, bento-dashboard chrome, generic SaaS cards, logos, or watermarks. Core controls and calendar content must look semantic and buildable with HTML/CSS. One dominant focal moment, quiet supporting structure.
