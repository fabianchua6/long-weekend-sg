Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity minimalist desktop website concept, landscape 1536×1024
Primary request: Create composition option C for “Long Weekend SG”, a simple interactive public-holiday planner for Singapore. It should feel friendly, light, and exceptionally clear.
Structure: a concise header row: title “Long Weekend SG” on the left, a small red badge “2027 official” beside it, one sentence under the title, and year/toggle controls aligned right. Under the header, a narrow “Best opportunities” strip shows three plain text summaries: “1 day → 4 off”, “2 days → 5 off”, “4 days → 9 off”. The main area is a four-column by three-row grid of twelve equal white month cards. The cards have rounded 12px corners and very thin grey borders. March is active; a small white popover is positioned inside its upper right corner with exact text “Take Friday off” and “4 days together”. Dates in the continuous break form a single pale-blue horizontal pill across one week row with the public holiday in red.
Content: Monday-first calendar grids, JAN through DEC, accurate-looking density, red holiday dots, pale blue leave days, optional school-break shading, minimal legend and source note.
Visual style: soft white/off-white background, white cards, near-black text, neutral grey secondary text, restrained Singapore red and pale sky blue only. System sans typography, clear numerals, generous whitespace, no decoration. Slightly friendlier and rounder than an enterprise dashboard.
Constraints: no dark mode, no airport or travel theme, no imagery, no logo, no textures, no illustrations, no props, no gradients, no glass, no heavy shadow, no marketing hero, no novelty typography, no fake claims, no watermark. Buildable with semantic HTML/CSS, accessible and calm.
